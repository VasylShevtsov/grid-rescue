GRID RESCUE — MILESTONE 3 DEMO AND MANUAL VERIFICATION

PURPOSE
Demonstrate the real controls, useful invalid-action feedback, cargo, upgrades,
mission outcome, and restart. This is a manual walkthrough, not an autoplay
feature. Each numbered route step is one successful action. Selection, Help,
and rejected operations do not spend actions.

PREPARATION
From the repository root, run:
  mvn clean verify
  java -jar target/grid-rescue-1.0-SNAPSHOT.jar

Fallback after mvn test:
  java -cp target/classes edu.whitman.ui.GridRescueApplication

Use a graphical desktop and a window large enough to show the board and unit
details. Rehearse the route before the presentation. The slide outline allocates
two minutes to the demo, so use the screenshots as backup if needed.

VERIFICATION EVIDENCE
- docs/Milestone_3_Verification.txt records the final clean-build results and

CHECK THE START
- Start a fresh mission; confirm restarting if an active mission has progress.
- Check 80 actions remaining, 0 used of 80 total actions, and 0 of 4 survivors
  home. The normal mission objective should be visible.
- Confirm Scout (5,0), Medic (5,1), RescueTruck (5,3), CommandUnit (5,4).
- Confirm survivors A (0,0), B (0,4), C (2,4), D (3,0); shelter (5,2).
- Check the square terrain tiles, distinct unit art, survivor markers, and
  selected-unit outline. Read the labels and legend as well as the colors.
- Open How to play and check the objective, controls, movement, terrain, cargo, upgrades,
  and win/loss explanation. Return to the mission; check that no action was
  spent. Press H to reopen Help and Escape to return.

CHECK SELECTION AND REJECTION
- Select Scout in the roster. Check capacity 1, empty cargo, 0 delivered, and
  its initial upgrade state. Legal destinations should be (4,0) and (4,1).
- Click Medic at (5,1). This selects Medic and spends no action. Press 1 to
  select Scout again; use 2–4 to inspect the other units, then return to Scout.
- Click (0,0), a distant destination. Expect an invalid-move message and
  unchanged position, cargo, rescue count, status, and 0 actions used.
- Check that Pick up survivor and Deliver cargo are unavailable at the starting
  position and that the selected-unit panel explains what to do next.
- Move focus to a map tile with Tab, then use arrow keys. Focus should move
  without moving the unit or spending actions. Activate a legal focused tile
  with Enter or Space, then start a fresh mission before the route below.

WINNING ROUTE
Coordinates are (row,column), with (0,0) at the upper-left corner. Click roster
buttons, occupied map tiles, or number keys to change units; click an empty
legal destination tile to move. Use Pick up survivor and Deliver cargo only at
the specified points. Clicking another unit selects it and does not request a move.

Scout: rescue D and earn the water upgrade.
  01  Move to (4,0)
  02  Move to (3,0)
      Check: D is still waiting; arrival did not automatically collect D.
      Pick up survivor becomes available; the next-step guidance points to it.
  03  Pick up D
      Check: cargo contains D; D no longer appears as a waiting survivor.
      The mission tracker distinguishes carried survivors from rescued ones.
  04  Move to (4,1)
  05  Move to (5,2)
  06  Deliver D
      Check: 1 rescued, cargo empty, 1 personal delivery, Scout upgraded,
      6 actions used and 74 remaining. Scout can now enter water but still
      cannot enter blocked terrain. Medic has not upgraded.

      Check: 2 rescued; Scout has 2 deliveries and retains its upgrade.

      Check: "Everyone made it home" and "Mission won", with 4 of 4 rescued,

CHECK TERMINAL BEHAVIOR AND RESTART
- Check the distinct success summary: four survivors rescued and 40 actions
  remaining. Gameplay controls must not change the completed mission.
- Choose Play again in the outcome summary. Check a fresh default board, all four waiting survivors,
  no cargo, no upgrades, 0 rescued, 80 remaining, and the normal mission objective.
- Make one legal move to show that the fresh mission is playable.
- Request a restart. Cancel it and check the same position, selection, cargo,
  and action count. Request a restart again and confirm it; check a clean reset.
- Open Help during play, return to the mission, and check that progress survives.

OPTIONAL LOSS CHECK (OUTSIDE THE TWO-MINUTE PRESENTATION)
- Start another fresh mission and select Scout.
- Alternate moves from (5,0) to (4,0) and back, 40 round trips / 80 actions.
  Both tiles are plain and unoccupied; no survivor is collected on this route.
- After action 80, check the "Mission lost" summary, 0 remaining, no legal moves, and no further
  gameplay changes. Start a fresh mission and confirm the reset again.
- Accepted model tests separately verify victory on the final permitted action
  and terminal exception precedence; no custom-map editor is exposed in the UI.
