BEHAVIOR CHECKS
- The published route wins in exactly 40 actions through the model, controller,
  and real Swing button events. The default mission retains 40 actions.
- The route also wins on the last action of a 40-action budget; it loses with a
  39-action budget. Terminal model actions remain locked.
- Invalid moves preserve state and spend no action. Subsequent valid actions
  succeed and replace the rejection feedback.
- Squad buttons, occupied map tiles, and 1–4 shortcuts select a unit for free.
- Pickup/delivery readiness and next-step text follow survivor arrival, cargo,
  shelter arrival, batch delivery, and upgrades. P/D respect disabled controls.
- Scout water access and Medic's second seat are exercised separately.
- Arrow bindings navigate map focus without acting; activation is explicit.
  Enter activates focused controls. Focus remains reachable after number-key
  selection. Headless binding tests invoke the real InputMap/ActionMap actions.
- Help opens and closes without losing progress. Gameplay shortcuts are blocked
  while Help or restart confirmation is open; Escape returns to the mission.
- Keep playing and Escape preserve the exact active game and cargo. Confirming
  restart creates fresh model objects and resets the board, actions, and upgrades.
- Win/loss summaries permit inspection but no gameplay actions. Play again
  creates a fresh playable default mission.
- Dynamic result, selection, and restart text remains visible after updates.
- Compact controls fit their layout; Help rules fit inside scrollable content;
  map tiles remain square at compact, default, and enlarged sizes.
- The original model, accepted tests, TestSupport, grading script, and CI
  workflow remain unchanged by this milestone.
